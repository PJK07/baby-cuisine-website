Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

At Baby Cuisine, we care deeply about the privacy of our customers and their little ones. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in the Baby Cuisine mobile app (Android package com.flowtomic.babycuisine) and on our website thebabycuisine.com.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require an account, and we do not collect payment or financial information in the app or on the website.

  • Information you enter in the app: your baby's first name and date of birth (used to suggest age-appropriate textures), your first-bites journal (foods tried, reactions and optional notes), and your basket. This data is stored only on your device. It is not sent to our servers or to any third party.
  • Website cart: on the website, your cart is saved in your browser's local storage so it is not lost on refresh. It stays on your device.
  • Website usage data: the website uses Vercel Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (such as pages viewed) to understand site performance. It does not identify you personally. The mobile app does not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

Baby Growth Tracker: the weight, length/height, age and sex you enter are used to calculate a growth percentile on your device. They are not stored or sent anywhere unless you choose "ask for nutrition guidance", which opens WhatsApp with those details pre-filled for you to send to us. If you send that message, we use it only to give you nutrition guidance. The tracker is for general information and is not medical advice.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

2. Ordering via WhatsApp

When you tap "Order on WhatsApp" in the app or on the website, your basket contents are turned into a text message and opened in WhatsApp, addressed to our official number (+961 70 465 465). Nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

Any delivery details (such as your name, address and phone number) are provided by you directly in the WhatsApp chat. That conversation is also governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy and terms of service.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information you share with us through WhatsApp is used only to:

  • Process, prepare and deliver your freshly handmade meals.
  • Contact you about your order status or delivery details.
  • Answer your questions and provide support.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your data. We only share information where needed to fulfil your order:

  • Delivery partners: we share your name, phone number and address with our local delivery courier so they can bring your baby's meals to you.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): used as the messaging channel for orders.
  • Vercel Analytics: anonymous website usage statistics only.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • In-app data: you can delete everything the app stores at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Order information: we keep order and delivery details only as long as needed to serve you. You can ask us to delete or correct your contact and address details at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp or emailing us.

6. Children's Privacy

Baby Cuisine is intended for use by parents and caregivers, not by children. The baby details you enter in the app (first name, date of birth and journal entries) stay on your device and are never collected by us.

7. Security

Our website is served over HTTPS. Because app data stays on your device, it is protected by your phone's own security settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
